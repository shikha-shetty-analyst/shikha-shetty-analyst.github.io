About me

I'm a data analyst based in Bangalore with hands-on experience in SQL, Power BI, Tableau and Excel across business finance, customer analytics and process improvement.

At Flipkart, I worked with the Business Finance (BGM) team on P&L and coupon-burn reporting, automating tracking that used to be done by hand and building a Power BI dashboard the business could use on its own. Before that, at Convin.ai, I analysed client reports and conversation data to find recurring operational issues and turn them into recommendations.

My work sits between data and the people who need it: collecting and validating data, building standardised and ad-hoc reports, and training non-technical stakeholders on data tools. I'm now looking for a Business Analyst role where I can work closely with business teams on the questions behind the numbers.
